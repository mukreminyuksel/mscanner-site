3. Main characteristics of the service

The free version includes unlimited scanning, automatic corner detection, cropping, basic filters, multi-page PDF, printing and sharing, and the PIN-protected hidden folder. mScanner Pro additionally unlocks:

  • Text recognition (OCR) and prompts with the AI on your device
  • Print Ready and advanced filters (including Remove Toner, book and shadow correction)
  • Signature, stamp and watermark
  • Batch actions on all pages
  • PDFs without the mScanner mark

Your documents are processed and stored on your device; Pro is not a cloud storage service.

5. Nature and duration of the agreement

  • The mScanner Pro subscription is an agreement of indefinite duration. The fee is charged in advance at the start of each billing period you choose (monthly or yearly), and the subscription continues until you terminate it. The period you choose is a billing period, not the term of the agreement.
  • Where a free trial is offered and it is not cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends, the paid period starts and the fee is charged to your store account. If you cancel during the trial, you are not charged.

6. Payment and performance

  • Confirming the purchase in the store's confirmation sheet creates an obligation to pay. The fee is charged at confirmation (on a plan with a trial, when the trial ends); later periods are charged at the start of each period.
  • Performance: Pro features unlock on your device as soon as the payment is confirmed. If they do not, tap Settings › Restore purchases or write to us.

7. Termination and refund of the remaining fee

You can terminate the subscription at any time, without giving a reason and without any penalty. There are two ways:

  • Cancel in the store: iPhone/iPad: Settings › Apple Account (your name) › Subscriptions › mScanner Pro › Cancel Subscription; Android: Google Play › profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › mScanner Pro › Cancel subscription. In the app, Settings › Manage subscription opens the same page. No further payment is taken and Pro stays on until the end of the paid period.
  • End it immediately and get the remaining fee back: if you want the subscription to end right away, without waiting for the end of the period, and the fee for the unused time refunded, you can also send your notice of termination by email to info@mscanner.com.tr.
  • Purchases on Google Play: we process your notice within 7 days at the latest, end the subscription and refund the amount for the unused time in full through Google Play within 15 days of termination. Including your order number (starting with “GPA.”) speeds this up.
  • Purchases on the App Store: only Apple can issue refunds. Under Apple's terms you can end the subscription immediately and get a pro-rated refund through Apple Support (in Türkiye also by calling 00800 4488 29878). If you write to us we will help you with this.
  • Deleting the app does not end the subscription.

8. Right of withdrawal

mScanner Pro is a service whose performance starts electronically and instantly once the purchase is confirmed. Under Art. 15(1)(ğ) of the Turkish Distance Contracts Regulation it is therefore excluded from the right of withdrawal. This exception does not affect your right to terminate and get the remaining fee back in section 7. In countries with different rules, such as the European Union, any withdrawal and refund rights that the store you bought from provides in that country remain unaffected.

  • If something goes wrong, write to us first; if we cannot fix it, we will give you what you need for a refund request.

10. Price changes

We may change the subscription fee only for a valid reason, such as a change in taxes or law, a change in the store's pricing rules or a significant increase in costs. A change is announced through the store and the app at least 30 days before it takes effect, and under the store's rules your consent is asked where required. The price of a period already paid does not change; if you do not accept the new price, you can terminate the subscription.

11. Compatibility, technical requirements and technical protection measures

  • iPhone and iPad: iOS / iPadOS 16.4 or later. Android: 8.0 or later.
  • Buying and verifying the subscription need an internet connection; scanning and your documents work offline.
  • Prompts that run on the phone's own AI work only on devices that support it (e.g. iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence; Android phones with Gemini Nano). On other devices the text is handed to the AI app you choose.
  • Technical protection measures: access to Pro is checked through the store account you bought with and subscription verification (RevenueCat). No copy protection is applied to the documents and PDFs you create.
  • The subscription belongs to the store account you bought it with; it can be restored on your devices that use the same store account. It does not move between Apple and Google.
