mScanner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · App version 2.31 and later (sections 5 and 8: from the first app update released after this date)

mScanner (“the app”) is a document scanner developed by MY İŞLETME DANIŞMANLIK SANAYİ VE TİCARET LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ. This policy explains what information the app processes and how.

Data controller: MY İŞLETME DANIŞMANLIK SANAYİ VE TİCARET LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ · ATATÜRK MAH. FEVZİ PAŞA CADDESİ, BÜYÜK ÇARŞI NO 35, KAT 3, NO:316, MERKEZ, ERZİNCAN 24050, TÜRKİYE · info@mscanner.com.tr · MERSİS 0627135184200001 · Erzincan Trade Registry no. 10080

In short

  • Your documents are processed and stored on your device. We run no server; your documents are never sent to us.
  • No account is needed. There are no ads, ad tracking or analytics tools.
  • The app itself never sends your documents to any AI server. For AI prompts you can, if you wish, hand the text to an AI app installed on your phone yourself.
  • If you buy a subscription, payment is handled by Apple or Google and subscription status by RevenueCat. The app contacts RevenueCat only when it is needed (section 5).
  • In US states whose law requires it, the app asks Apple or Google for your age range; the answer stays on your device (section 8).

1. Documents and scanning

Pages you capture or import, crop/filter results, PDFs, notes, text recognition (OCR) results, signatures and watermarks are stored in the app's private folder on your device. Edge detection, filters and text recognition run on the device.

  • Android: text recognition and the “Device AI scanner” use Google ML Kit / Google Play services. To operate these, Google may collect technical information such as device model, OS version, app package name, a per-installation identifier and performance/error metrics (Google ML Kit data disclosure). Your document images and text are not part of these metrics.
  • iOS: text recognition and the document scanner use Apple's on-device Vision/VisionKit frameworks.
  • The app does not add its own encryption to documents; your device's encryption and lock screen apply. If device backup (iCloud / Google) is on, app data including documents may be included in the operating system's backup; the PIN digest (secure key store) is not backed up.
  • When you share, print or save a document to Photos or a folder, it goes to the destination you choose.

2. Permissions

  • Camera: to scan documents and QR/barcodes. Images stay on the device.
  • Photos: to pick images to scan and, when you ask, to save pages to Photos. On Android the system photo picker is used: the app sees only the images you pick and never asks for access to all your photos.
  • Internet: subscriptions (RevenueCat and the store, only when needed; section 5), opening links, and on Android downloading the ML Kit text recognition/scanner components and the on-device AI model through Google Play services, plus the technical metrics Google collects for those components (below). The content of your documents is not sent.
  • Face ID / fingerprint / device lock: only to confirm it's you when resetting the hidden folder's PIN; no biometric data reaches the app.

3. PIN-protected hidden folder

Your PIN is never stored in plain text: a salted, iterated SHA-256 digest is kept in the device's secure key store (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore), bound to this device. Repeated wrong attempts trigger a waiting period. The hidden folder locks again when the app goes to the background, and while hidden content is on screen Android blocks screenshots and the recent-apps preview. If you forget the PIN or its record cannot be read (e.g. after restoring a backup), a new PIN can only be set after you confirm it's you with your phone's own lock (face, fingerprint or device passcode). The PIN hides documents inside the app; it is not an encrypted vault. Because documents are included in device backups, someone who restores the backup and can unlock that phone can reset the PIN and see hidden documents; “hidden” is not protection against backups.

4. AI prompts (with the app on your phone)

On the “OCR & Mobile AI” screen the document text and a prompt prepared for the task you choose are copied to the clipboard, and the AI app installed on your phone that you pick (e.g. ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) is opened. Pasting, sending and bringing the answer back is up to you; that app's own terms and privacy policy apply. mScanner itself sends no data to any AI server and asks for no API key. Any API key or permission stored by an older version is deleted from the device at launch.

The phone's own AI: If your phone has an on-device language model (Apple Intelligence on iOS 26 and later; Gemini Nano through Google's AICore service on some Android phones), the prompt runs on that model on the phone and the text does not leave it. On iOS only the on-device model is used, never Apple's server side (Private Cloud Compute). On Android the model may be downloaded by the system on first use, and Google may collect technical metrics to operate the service (Google ML Kit data disclosure).

5. Subscriptions and purchases

If you buy mScanner Pro, payment is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; your card details never reach us. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to verify subscription status. RevenueCat processes an automatically generated pseudonymous app user ID that does not contain your name and purchase history (product, transaction, date, store) (RevenueCat privacy policy). We do not ask for your name or email.

The app contacts RevenueCat only when it is needed: when you open the mScanner Pro screen, tap “Restore purchases” or “Support ID” in Settings, when this device had Pro before, or for up to 30 days while a purchase waits for approval (for example a parent's) or a gift to your support ID is expected. If you never do any of these, the app sends nothing to RevenueCat and no RevenueCat record exists for you.

If a purchase needs someone's approval (for example a parent's), the store keeps it pending; Pro turns on by itself once it is approved.

6. What we do not share

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it or share it with anyone for advertising, targeted advertising or profiling (there is no “sale” or “sharing” within the meaning of US state privacy laws). We do not transfer it to third parties other than the service providers named above, except where the law requires (and since no documents are on our servers, we hold no document content to disclose).

7. Retention and deletion

  • Documents on your device: stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app; they never reach us.
  • Subscription records (RevenueCat): created only when the app contacts RevenueCat (section 5); kept while the subscription is active and for at most 3 years after it ends (a record without a purchase: at most 3 years after it was last used), then deleted; deleted earlier if you ask.
  • Age range (section 8): not kept. Only the time of the last request stays on your device, so the app does not ask again within a year.
  • The stores' payment and sales reports (order number, product, amount, country; no name or card details): kept for 10 years as required by Turkish commercial and tax law (Law No. 6563 Art. 11, Turkish Commercial Code Art. 82, Tax Procedure Law Art. 253).
  • Support emails: kept for at most 3 years after the request is closed.
  • Website logs: subject to GitHub's own retention policy.
  • The subscription is managed or cancelled in your store account settings. Detailed deletion steps, records that are not deleted and why: https://mscanner.com.tr/data-deletion.html

8. Children and the age range from the store

mScanner is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13. It has no age-restricted content and works the same for every age; for minors, the store handles parental consent for downloads and purchases.

Age range from the store (US): some US states require apps to ask the app store or the operating system for the user's age range (Texas, and from 1 January 2027 California and Alabama; Utah and Louisiana later). Where such a law applies, the app asks Apple (Declared Age Range) or Google Play (Age Signals) for the age range — under 13, 13–15, 16–17 or 18 and over — when it is first opened and again at most once a year. The answer stays in the app's memory on your device. It is not stored, not sent to us, to RevenueCat or to anyone else, and not used for advertising, profiling or analytics; it is used only to comply with the law. On iPhone and iPad the app asks only when Apple reports that such a law applies to your account; on Android, only when your phone's region or time zone is in the United States.

Children under 13: we do not collect personal data from children for our own purposes. If the store reports that a user is under 13 and that user opens the subscription screen, restores purchases or buys with a parent's approval, RevenueCat receives only the pseudonymous app user ID and the purchase records described in section 5. These are used only to provide the subscription — verifying and restoring it, unlocking Pro and answering support requests — and for the network connection this needs. They are not used to contact the child, to build a profile, for advertising or for any other purpose; RevenueCat processes them only on our instructions as our service provider, and the app contains no advertising or analytics tools. A parent can ask us to delete this record at info@mscanner.com.tr (section 7).

9. Notice under Turkish law (KVKK) and your rights

This section is our notice as data controller under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

  • Personal data we process: Your documents never reach us, so we do not process them. We process only: if the app contacts RevenueCat (when you open the subscription screen or buy a subscription; section 5), the pseudonymous app user ID and purchase records in RevenueCat (product, transaction/order number, date, store, store country); if you email us, your email address and the content of the correspondence; if you visit our website, the technical logs kept by the hosting provider (section 11).
  • Purposes and legal bases:
  • Verifying the subscription, unlocking Pro and handling termination and refund requests: establishing and performing a contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c).
  • Keeping commercial and tax records: legal obligation (Art. 5/2-ç).
  • Establishing, exercising or defending a right (e.g. disputes): Art. 5/2-e.
  • Questions, support and complaints, and serving the website securely: our legitimate interest, provided it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms (Art. 5/2-f).
  • No processing relies on explicit consent.
  • How it is collected: automatically (electronically) through the app and the store; when you send an email; through server logs when you visit the website.
  • Recipients and transfers abroad: Data is shared only with these service providers and only for the purposes above: RevenueCat, Inc. (USA; subscription verification, processor), Zoho (email; EU data centre, processor), GitHub, Inc. (USA; website hosting). Apple and Google are separate controllers that handle the purchase through your own account. Their servers are outside Türkiye, so this data is transferred abroad.
  • Automated decisions: no decision about you is based solely on automated processing, and no profiling takes place.
  • Your rights (KVKK Art. 11): to learn whether your personal data is processed; to request information if it is; to learn the purpose and whether it is used accordingly; to know the third parties it is transferred to in Türkiye or abroad; to request correction if it is incomplete or wrong; to request erasure or destruction under KVKK Art. 7; to request that corrections and erasures be notified to those third parties; to object to an outcome against you that results solely from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.
  • How to apply: Send your application in Turkish (a) as a signed letter to our company address, or (b) signed with a secure electronic signature or mobile signature to info@mscanner.com.tr, or (c) from your own registered e-mail (KEP) address to our KEP address myisletmedanismanlik@hs01.kep.tr. It should include your name, your signature for a written application, your Turkish ID number (or nationality and passport number if you are a foreign national), your address for notifications, your email or phone if any, and your request. For simple requests, such as deleting the RevenueCat record, an email with your order number or the Settings › Support ID shown in the app is enough.
  • Response time and complaints: Applications are answered within 30 days at the latest, as a rule free of charge (KVKK Art. 13). If your application is rejected, you find the answer insufficient or it is not answered in time, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board within 30 days of learning the answer and in any case within 60 days of your application (KVKK Art. 14).

Because your documents are only on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app.

11. Website

mscanner.com.tr is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open the site your browser connects to GitHub's servers; GitHub may keep technical logs such as IP address, date/time and browser details for security and operation (GitHub privacy statement). The site has no cookies, analytics, ads, or fonts or scripts loaded from other servers; fonts are served by the site itself. The language you pick is stored only in your browser (localStorage) and is not sent to us, so no cookie consent is asked.

12. Security

Communication with service providers uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to subscription records and support emails is limited to the company's authorised person. Because your documents never reach us, there is no risk of a document leak on our side; security on your device depends on your device lock and the operating system's protection.
