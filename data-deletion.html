mScanner — Data Deletion

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · App version 2.31 and later

This page explains how to delete data related to mScanner (Mobile Document Scanner), developed by MY İŞLETME DANIŞMANLIK SANAYİ VE TİCARET LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ. mScanner has no accounts and your documents are not sent to our servers, so you delete most data yourself, directly on your device.

1. Data on your device — you delete it

  • Documents, page images, PDFs, text recognition (OCR) results, notes, signatures and watermarks: deleted when you delete the document in the app or uninstall the app. This data never reaches us; there is no copy on our side to delete.
  • Device backups: on Android app data may be included in the phone's Google backup, on iPhone/iPad in the iCloud device backup. You delete these backups in your own Google or Apple account's backup settings; we have no access to them.
  • PIN: only a salted digest is kept in the device's secure key store. On Android it is removed when the app is uninstalled. On iOS, Keychain items can remain on the device after the app is removed; that item contains only the PIN digest, no documents. Resetting the device removes it too.

2. Subscription record (RevenueCat) — ask us to delete it

The app contacts RevenueCat — the service we use to verify subscriptions — only when it is needed: when you open the mScanner Pro screen, tap “Restore purchases” or “Support ID”, or buy. Then RevenueCat holds a pseudonymous app user ID that does not contain your name and, if you bought, the purchase history (product, transaction/order ID, date, store, country and currency). Your name, email address and card details are not in this record. If you did none of these, the app sends nothing to RevenueCat. Versions before 3.33 connected at every start, so if you used them an older record may exist; you can ask us to delete it the same way.

How to request deletion:

  1. Email info@mscanner.com.tr with the subject “mScanner data deletion”.
  2. So that we can find your record safely, include only one of these:
  • your Google Play order number (starts with “GPA.”) — in Google's purchase email or at play.google.com › Payments & subscriptions › Budget & history;
  • your Apple Order ID — in Apple's receipt email or at reportaproblem.apple.com;
  • if you never made a purchase: the Support ID shown in the app under Settings › Subscription;
  • if you have neither (for example on a device where the app no longer shows the Support ID): the country, the phone's operating system and roughly when the Pro screen was used. We look for matching records that contain no purchase and delete them, and tell you if we cannot find any.
  1. Do not send card numbers, passwords, ID documents or other personal details; we do not ask for them.

Parents can ask in the same way to review or delete the record of a child's device, or to stop using it; we use reasonable checks to verify a request. If the store reports that a user is under 13, the app does not connect to RevenueCat on that device at all, so no new record is created for that user. On other devices the app connects only in the cases described in the privacy policy (section 5): if Pro was never on there, not using the Pro screen, “Restore purchases” and “Support ID” stops future collection within 30 days at most, and uninstalling the app stops it in every case. If we learn that a record belongs to a child under 13 without a parent's verifiable consent, we delete it within 30 days at the latest.

We use that number or ID to find the pseudonymous record in RevenueCat, delete it with RevenueCat's customer deletion function, and reply to you from the same address. Under Turkish data protection law (KVKK, Law No. 6698) requests are answered within 30 days at the latest.

Records that are not deleted or may remain, and why:

  • Google Play's and the App Store's own sales records: the stores make the sale and keep their records under their own policies; we cannot delete them. You can contact Google or Apple about them.
  • RevenueCat's backups: a deleted record may remain for a while in RevenueCat's backups under RevenueCat's standard backup and archival practices; RevenueCat does not publish how long (RevenueCat data processing addendum). We do not restore or use it.
  • Payment and accounting records: payout reports the stores send us (order/transaction details, product, amount, country) are kept for the periods required by tax and commercial law. They do not contain your name or card details.
  • If your subscription is still active: deletion does not cancel it. When the app asks the store for the subscription status, a new pseudonymous record is created in RevenueCat.

3. Google's technical metrics (Android)

On Android, text recognition, the document scanner and on-device AI components run through Google Play services. Google may collect device and app information, a per-installation identifier and performance/error metrics for these components (Google ML Kit data disclosure). This data is not passed to us and we cannot delete it; Google's policies apply. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data resets the installation identifier.

4. Cancelling a subscription and deleting data are separate

  • To cancel the subscription: Google Play › Subscriptions, or on iPhone/iPad Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Cancelling does not delete your data.
  • Deleting data does not cancel the subscription or give a refund. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription either.
